3. What is ESS60 (Carbon 60)? The Real Science for Dogs

Now that we know exactly what is in the bottle, does ESS60 actually help dogs?

ESS60 is a highly purified, safe-for-consumption grade of Carbon-60 (C60). In scientific literature, C60 suspended in olive oil is recognized as an immensely powerful cellular antioxidant—often cited as being significantly more potent than Vitamin C in neutralizing oxidative stress.

However, honesty is required here: while reducing systemic oxidative stress naturally aids in reducing overall inflammation (which can help a senior dog move better), there are currently no massive, long-term clinical trials specifically proving that ESS60 acts as a targeted structural joint repair agent like traditional cartilage-builders.

It is safe, it is a fantastic antioxidant, but it is not a direct replacement for clinically prescribed osteoarthritis medication.
