3. Debunking The NASA & "BioSignal" Claims

The sales funnel relies heavily on emotional storytelling. They mention a "melted chocolate" memory trick, a retired NASA engineer named Frank Shepherd, and a biological process involving a "calcified shell" and restoring a "BioSignal".

Marketing Fiction vs. Medical Fact

According to consumer protection sites like ScamAdviser, terms like "BioSignal" and "calcified shell" in this context are not recognized medical diagnoses or established physiological mechanisms. Furthermore, the images used to suggest television appearances for "Frank Shepherd" show signs of being AI-generated or manipulated. You should view this narrative purely as a marketing device to sell supplements, not as a medically verified documentary.
