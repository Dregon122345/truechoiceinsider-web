Debunking The "83% Sugar" Marketing Claim

One of the most aggressive claims made by Advanced Amino Formula is that 83% of whey protein is turned into sugar (glucose) and metabolic waste, leaving only 17% for muscle building.

This is a manipulation of a process called gluconeogenesis. While it is true that the body will convert excess dietary protein into glucose if it doesn't need it for muscle repair, framing this as "whey turns into sugar" is a scare tactic. Whey protein remains one of the highest-scoring biological proteins on the planet.

However, the core premise of their pitch is scientifically accurate: Free-form amino acids like those in Advanced Amino Formula boast a Nitrogen Utilization (NNU) rate of up to 99%. This means virtually zero nitrogen waste is filtered through your kidneys, making EAAs incredibly safe and efficient for aging bodies.
