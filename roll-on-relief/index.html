1. Potential Drawbacks

Before deciding whether to buy a topical product, consider its scent, packaging, application method, ingredients and price.

  • Scent: Menthol-containing topical products can have a strong medicinal smell. Check the actual product label and your own experience before making a claim about this item.
  • Applicator: A rollerball applicator can be convenient, but check that it moves freely and that the product is applied as directed.

2. Application and Experience

Topical pain-relief products may provide temporary sensations such as cooling or warming, depending on their active ingredients. Individual results vary.

Do not interpret a cooling or warming sensation as proof that a product treats the underlying cause of pain. Follow the package directions and consult a healthcare professional about persistent or severe symptoms.

3. Pros and Cons Breakdown

Potential Advantages

  • Rollerball format may simplify application.
  • Portable packaging may be convenient.
  • Topical application avoids handling a separate cream applicator.

Things to Check

  • Scent and skin sensitivity.
  • Full ingredient list and directions.
  • Shipping costs, delivery time and return policy.

4. Ingredients and Safety

Check the product's actual ingredient label to identify its active ingredients, concentration, warnings and directions. Do not assume that a product is safe for everyone simply because it contains plant-derived ingredients.

Use topical products only as directed. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes, and do not apply to damaged skin unless the label specifically permits it. Stop using the product if a reaction occurs and seek medical advice when needed.
